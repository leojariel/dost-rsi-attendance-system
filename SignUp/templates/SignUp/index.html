About this event

The 8th Research, Statistics, and Innovation Forum is a premier gathering organized by the Department of Science and Technology (DOST), bringing together researchers, statisticians, data scientists, and innovators from across the Philippines. This three-day forum serves as a national platform for the exchange of ideas, presentation of cutting-edge research, and discussion of emerging trends in statistics and innovation-driven development.

The event features a comprehensive program designed to foster interdisciplinary collaboration and capacity-building among participants. It highlights the critical role of research and statistics in shaping evidence-based policies and sustainable solutions for national development.

Objectives:
  • Provide a platform for researchers and statisticians to present and discuss their latest findings.
  • Promote collaboration between academic institutions, government agencies, and industry partners.
  • Showcase innovative solutions and statistical methodologies that address real-world challenges.
  • Enhance the capacity of participants through workshops, plenary sessions, and technical discussions.
  • Advocate the importance of data-driven decision-making in policy formulation and program implementation.
